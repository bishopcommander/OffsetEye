DAILY DRILLING REPORT (DDR) #48 - OFFSHORE SECTOR
WELL: 15/9-F-12
OPERATOR: STATOIL / EQUINOR
DATE: 14-OCT-2008
CURRENT OPERATION: DRILLING 8-1/2 INCH HOLE SECTION
MEASURED DEPTH: 2930.5 M MD (TVD: 2855.0 M)
CURRENT FORMATION: HUGIN SANDSTONE RESERVOIR

OPERATIONAL NARRATIVE & INCIDENT SUMMARY:
At 04:30 hrs, while drilling ahead at 2930.5m MD with 1.34 SG oil-based mud, observed instantaneous drop in active pit volume of 6.2 m3 within 12 minutes. Dynamic mud losses established at 34 m3/hr.
Standpipe pressure decreased from 195 bar to 160 bar with penetration into high-permeability, partially depleted Hugin sand.

MITIGATION & CORRECTIVE ACTIONS:
1. Stopped drilling and pulled string off bottom to 2910m.
2. Mixed and spotted 18 m3 high-fluid-loss crosslinked LCM pill containing 35 ppb medium calcium carbonate, mica flakes, and cellulose fibers.
3. Allowed pill to hesitate squeeze for 90 minutes with 15 bar backpressure.
4. Resumed circulation at reduced flow rate of 1750 l/min. Losses reduced to negligible seepage (< 1.5 m3/hr).
5. Formation tops: Top Hugin at 2900m MD to 3210m MD. Top Shetland at 2450m MD.
